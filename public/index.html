What I lead

Owner’s Representation

Represent ownership throughout the project, serving as the link between owners, architects, consultants, and contractors. Keep schedule, budget, quality, and execution aligned with ownership’s objectives.

Project Leadership

Provide hands-on leadership when projects require stronger direction and coordination. Oversee schedules, field operations, contractors, quality control, procurement, and sequencing, while maintaining clear communication and accountability to ownership.

Closeout & Turnover

The final stages of a project demand just as much leadership as the initial planning. Drive completion of outstanding finish work and punch lists, coordinate owners and contractors, and manage the process through turnover and Temporary Certificate of Occupancy (TCO).

Luxury Interiors & Complex Finishes

Bring specialized experience in high-end interiors and complex finishes, including stone, tile, marble, custom slabs, mosaics, and flooring. Manage the coordination, sequencing, and execution of the details that define the finished product and shape the owner’s and buyer’s experience.
