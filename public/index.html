How I can help

Owner’s representation

I represent you in the room. I coordinate architects, consultants and contractors, and I protect your schedule, budget and quality.

Senior project oversight

When a project needs more leadership, I step in. I run schedules, field conditions, contractors, quality, procurement and sequencing, and I report clearly to ownership.

Closeout & turnover

The last ten percent is the hardest. I drive remaining finish work and punch lists, coordinate owner and contractor, and push through turnover and the temporary certificate of occupancy (TCO).

Luxury interiors & complex finishes

I specialize in high-end interiors: stone, tile, marble, custom slabs, mosaics and flooring. These are the details clients see.
